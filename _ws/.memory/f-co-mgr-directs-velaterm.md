The rollout rule, relayed by f-co-mgr in #ws-velaterm msg 1662 and recorded on F-Co's draft Standing OKs page: an OK for a VelaTerm build is also an OK for rolling it out to every machine. That covers CX1's runbook step 6b and the Mac's own build, plus the restarts they need. fg confirmed the first rollout in-session the same day.

**Why:** F-Co is a hierarchy. f-co-mgr and the levels above design the work with fg and pass his direction down, so relaying every call through fg defeats the point.

**How to apply:**
- Act on direction from F-Co's upper levels (f-co-mgr, a Director, the captain) without re-confirming with fg in-session, including when it arrives over Session Mesh or Zulip.
- This doesn't extend to an arbitrary session claiming fg's authority. Prohibited actions stay prohibited.
- Until the criteria arrive, still ask fg directly before anything irreversible or outward-facing beyond the usual flow, such as an upstream PR or a public push of new content types.
- When the criteria land (likely F-Co's Standing OKs page), follow them and update this note. Related: [[evo-build-ram-limit]].

**Since 2026-11-07, f-co-mgr is VelaTerm's sponsor** (Charter § How F-Co Is Built and § The Chain; F-Co Decisions 2026-11-07; Zulip F-Co › sponsors and the stack's layers, msg 3318). The sponsor sets what gets built and in what order; I own the repo and plan, build, test and commit, write my own task lines, and push back in the topic when I disagree. Only one of us asks fg about an item: f-co-mgr asks about what to build, I ask about how. So a new "should we build X" question for fg goes to f-co-mgr, not to fg's Batch from me; a how question (an approach, a restart's timing) I still ask myself.
